About Mysaa Rituals

Mysaa Rituals creates handcrafted candles and fragrance-led gifts inspired by Indian memories, familiar rituals and the feeling a scent can leave behind.

Our Fragrances

Explore Gulab Ki Chitthi, Dhoop & Chandan, Gajre Ka Shringar, Madhuban, Raat Ki Rani and Saanjh across our candle formats and gifting collections.

Our Process

Our rituals are made in small batches with natural soy wax, cotton wicks and phthalate-free fragrance oils. We focus on thoughtful presentation, personalisation and gifting moments.
